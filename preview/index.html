01 / ABOUT

A continuous research story.

一条连续的研究脉络。

I am a Youth Researcher at Shanghai AI Laboratory and received my Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2026.

My work asks how intelligent systems can acquire knowledge and capability they were not explicitly given. It began with geometry, domain knowledge, multimodal structure, and physical priors; it now moves toward agents that learn from tools, environments, and verifiable feedback.

“From knowledge-guided foundation models to experience-driven self-evolving agents.”

我是上海人工智能实验室青年研究员，2026 年获上海交通大学博士学位。

我的研究始终关注：智能系统如何获得初始模型并不具备的知识与能力。它从几何、领域、多模态与物理先验出发，进一步走向从工具、环境和可验证反馈中持续学习的智能体。

“从知识驱动的基础模型，走向经验驱动的自进化智能体。”
